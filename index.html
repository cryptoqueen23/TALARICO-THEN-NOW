James Talarico:
Then & Now

A documented look at how his campaign website presented his politics before and during the 2025 U.S. Senate launch.

COMPARE THE RECORD
WAYBACK MACHINE

What changed?

Archived versions of jamestalarico.com preserve a detailed policy platform from 2024 and early 2025. By August 2025, the homepage's detailed ISSUES section was no longer displayed. By September 9, 2025, the site had been rebuilt around the statewide Senate campaign.

01

March 4, 2024

2024 PLATFORM

Talarico's campaign described this part of his record as “Bold, Progressive Legislation.” The archived site included an extensive issue-by-issue platform.

Health Care

Called health care a “human right,” supported Medicaid expansion and a Medicaid buy-in/public option, and described guaranteed health care as the eventual goal.

Abortion

Supported restoring Roe-era protections, protecting Planned Parenthood, expanding family planning, and putting abortion rights before Texas voters.

Gun Policy

Supported universal background checks and banning the sale of assault weapons.

Immigration

Supported DREAMers, ending indefinite detention, repealing SB 4, opposed out-of-state tuition for undocumented youth, and opposed state funding for a border wall.

Marijuana

Supported full legalization, expungement of past marijuana convictions, and using resulting revenue for early-childhood education.

Labor

Supported collective bargaining and repeal of Texas right-to-work laws, which his campaign called “right-to-work-for-less.”

Climate

Supported greenhouse-gas reduction targets, efficiency standards, and teaching climate change in schools.

Criminal Justice

Supported bail reform, reduced incarceration, restorative justice, marijuana legalization, and divestment from privately owned prisons.

Education

Promoted scientifically accurate sex education, social-emotional learning, restorative justice, and universal full-day early childhood education.

Social Issues

Identified as an LGBTQ+ ally, opposed Republican legislation concerning transgender minors, and opposed mandatory Ten Commandments displays in classrooms.

Voting

Said he led Democratic legislators in leaving Texas to break quorum and backed online voter registration, expanded early voting, and more polling locations.

Housing & Transit

Said “Housing is a human right” and supported affordable-housing efforts, high-speed rail, and expanded public transportation.

02

August 7, 2025

2025 CHANGES

Earlier navigation

DONATE · ACCOMPLISHMENTS · ISSUES · ENDORSEMENTS

August 2025 navigation

DONATE · ACCOMPLISHMENTS · ENDORSEMENTS

The detailed Issues platform was no longer displayed on the homepage recovered from the August 7 capture. However, the page still retained explicit language including “Bold, Progressive Legislation,” marijuana legalization, climate legislation, anti-union-law repeal, references to transgender minors, and criticism of a “Christian Nationalist agenda.”

What this proves: the campaign's website presentation changed. What it does not prove: why the campaign made the change or that Talarico renounced the underlying positions.
03

September 9, 2025

SENATE SITE

NEW STATEWIDE MESSAGE

“It’s time to Start Flipping Tables.”

The new campaign homepage emphasized an eighth-generation Texan, former middle-school teacher, Presbyterian seminarian, fighting billionaire mega-donors, corruption, a “rigged system,” and winning power back for working people.

The extensive issue-by-issue platform seen in the earlier homepage was no longer the centerpiece of the new Senate presentation.
